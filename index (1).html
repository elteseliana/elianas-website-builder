01 / about

A developer who sweats the details.

I'm Eliana — a front-end developer focused on building websites that are quick, accessible, and a pleasure to use. I care about the small things: how a button feels, how fast a page loads, how clean the markup is underneath.

I work end to end — taking an idea from a rough sketch to a polished, deployed site. Everything you see on this page was written by hand and shipped to Vercel.

When I'm not coding, I'm usually reading, sketching layouts, or pulling apart sites I admire to figure out what makes them tick.
