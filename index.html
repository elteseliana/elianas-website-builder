How I work

A website shouldn't leave you paying every time you want to change a sentence. Here's how I do it instead.

  1. 01

    A first draft, free

    Tell me about your business. I'll design a first version of your homepage at no cost, so you can see what you'd get before you decide.

  2. 02

    Built for you, owned by you

    Your words, your photos, your colors. The finished site and its accounts are in your name, not mine.

  3. 03

    I teach you to run it

    I'll walk you through updating it yourself. If you'd rather hand changes to me, I'm happy to stay on for those too.
