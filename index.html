01 / about

Hi, I'm Eliana.

I'm a 16-year-old web developer who builds clean, fast websites for small businesses — and I love every part of it. What started as curiosity about how websites work has turned into something I take seriously.

I build custom designs, not cookie-cutter templates. Every site is hand-coded to be quick, easy to use, and to look great on a phone or a computer. And because I'm just getting started, I keep my work genuinely affordable for businesses finding their feet.
