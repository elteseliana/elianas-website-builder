Our Kitchen

Cooked over open flame, served without fuss.

Ember & Oak started with a single wood-fired grill and a belief that the best food is simple, seasonal, and shared. We source from local farms and let the fire do the talking.

Pull up a chair at the bar, order a little of everything, and stay a while.
