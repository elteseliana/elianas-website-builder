Case study

Tzeida Laderech

A website for a national memorial initiative. Teenage volunteers travel to the families of fallen IDF soldiers, listen, and record their stories. The site explains the idea and signs up both volunteers and families.

ClientTamar & Bella, founders
My roleDesign, development, launch
LanguagesHebrew & English
Live sitetzeida-laderech.vercel.app ↗

The brief

Tamar and Bella needed one place to explain Tzeida Laderech and bring in two very different groups: teenagers who want to volunteer, and bereaved families who are willing to share their loved one's story.

Because of the subject, the tone mattered more than anything. It had to feel warm and respectful, never like a campaign. The site also had to work equally well in Hebrew and English, and on a phone, where most visitors would first see it.

What I built

  • A full Hebrew and English siteOne button switches the language, and the whole layout flips between right-to-left and left-to-right.
  • A map made of facesThe homepage map of Israel fills with portraits, so the scale of the project is seen rather than just stated.
  • Two sign-up pathsSeparate forms for volunteers and for families, each worded for who it's speaking to.
  • Direct contact with the foundersWhatsApp links to Tamar and Bella, so a family can reach a real person straight away.
  • A soft, painted lookSand and sea-blue tones with a watercolor-paper texture, chosen to suit a memorial project.

See it live

The site is live and in use. Have a look, in either language.
