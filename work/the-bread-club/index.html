Case study

The Bread Club

My own sourdough bakery in Ra'anana. I bake to order twice a week, so the website had to do more than look good. It had to take orders properly.

ClientMy own business
My roleBaking, photos, design, development
LanguagesEnglish & Hebrew
Live sitethebreadclub.github.io ↗

The problem

Orders used to come in through a Google Form. Keeping track of who ordered what, for which day, and whether there was still room in the oven took a lot of checking by hand.

I wanted customers to see the menu, choose a bake day and order in under a minute, in English or Hebrew. And I wanted every order to land in one place I could trust.

What I built

  • An order page that knows the rulesCustomers choose Tuesday or Friday. Each day closes at its cutoff time and stops taking orders once all its loaves are booked.
  • Prices calculated for youThe total, including the delivery fee where it applies, is worked out on the page, so nobody has to do the maths.
  • Orders go straight into a Google SheetEvery order lands in my spreadsheet with a number, and repeat taps don't create duplicates. The sheet adds up how many loaves of each flavor to bake.
  • English and HebrewOne button switches the whole site, including the order form, between the two languages.
  • Tested before it went liveAutomated tests check prices, cutoffs, the loaf limit and the full ordering flow in a real browser.
